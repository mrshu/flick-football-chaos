Adaptive engine

Six children of fixed ability on the same 1–8 scale, each starting at band 4. Their chance of knowing an answer falls off as the difficulty passes their ability, and whatever they do not know they guess from the choices on offer — which is what makes the two- and three-choice floor at the bottom of the scale do any work.

The engine should park each of them near 80% correct, at a band close to their ability. This is the same learner model test.js asserts against, so the two cannot disagree; the lab just lets you watch it rather than take its word.
