# Flick Football Chaos — Over-12 Extension Design

Date: 2026-08-07
Status: approved design, ready for implementation planning
Builds on: 2026-08-01-maths-mode-design.md (all its constraints still hold)

## 1. Purpose

Extend the game upward so a 16-year-old or an adult has real maths to play
for, without walling them off from the existing game. Today the ladder tops
out at band 8 (age ~12: negatives, squares, roots, one-step equations). This
design adds three bands above it and a presentation that does not read as a
little kid's game, while leaving everything below band 9 untouched.

Approved direction (chosen over a separate adult track and over an open-ended
continuous difficulty): **extend the existing ladder with discrete bands
9–11**, mixed-topic per band exactly like bands 1–8, driven by the same
adaptive engine.

## 2. Goals

- Bands 9, 10, 11 covering three content families: **algebra & symbols**,
  **geometry & visual**, **patterns & sequences**.
- A **harder answer format** at high difficulty: more choices, closer
  distractors, tighter (still invisible) expected-time bar.
- A **broadcast-style "pro" skin** for teams whose home band is 9+ —
  restrained palette and styling, identical layout and physics.
- A **form rating (47–99)** derived from the adaptive difficulty, plus
  personal bests (fastest correct answer, longest correct streak), shown on
  the stats card.
- A strong 12-year-old roams up into band 9+ naturally; a rusty adult placed
  at band 10 can slip down. One continuous difficulty number, as today.

## 3. Non-goals

- No estimation / real-world / word-problem content (explicitly declined —
  and word problems were already ruled out by the base spec).
- No logic-grid puzzles in this version: they need a new interaction model,
  not just a new render token. Sequences carry the patterns family.
- No typed/keypad answers; multiple choice stays (declined in favour of the
  harder-choice format).
- No risk/wager mechanic (declined).
- No visible timers — time pressure remains invisible, via `expectedMs`.
- No change to bands 1–8 content, the prize mechanic, or the 80% target.
- No accounts, networking, leaderboards. Ratings and bests are local.

## 4. Content: bands 9–11

Same generator contract as bands 1–8: a pure function of `rand` returning
`{ render, answer, skill, near }`. Every answer is a single whole number —
enforced by construction (Pythagorean triples, integer-solution equations,
degree answers, whole-number sequence terms). `near` misses are believable
mis-computations, not random jitter.

### Band 9 (~13–14)

| skill | family | example | answer |
|---|---|---|---|
| `eqn2` | algebra | `3x + 4 = 19`, find `x` (a·x ± b = c, x in 2–12) | 5 |
| `expand` | algebra | `4(x + 3) = 4x + □` | 12 |
| `angleLine` | geometry | drawn straight line split in two, one angle labelled, find the other (sum 180°) | 65 |
| `seqRule` | patterns | linear sequence with the gap mid-sequence: `7, □, 17, 22` | 12 |

### Band 10 (~15–16)

| skill | family | example | answer |
|---|---|---|---|
| `simul` | algebra | `x + y = 11`, `x − y = 3`, find `x` (sum/difference form, integer x > y ≥ 1) | 7 |
| `angleTri` | geometry | drawn triangle, two angles labelled, find the third (sum 180°) | 40 |
| `pythag` | geometry | drawn right triangle, legs labelled, find the hypotenuse — scaled Pythagorean triples only | 10 |
| `seqQuad` | patterns | `2, 5, 10, 17, □` (growing differences) | 26 |

### Band 11 (16+ ceiling)

| skill | family | example | answer |
|---|---|---|---|
| `indices` | algebra | `2³ × 2⁴ = 2^□` (add/subtract exponents) | 7 |
| `ineq` | algebra | largest whole `x` with `4x < 30` | 7 |
| `seqGeo` | patterns | `3, 6, 12, 24, □` (doubling/tripling, incl. one mid-gap variant) | 48 |
| `areaComp` | geometry | drawn L-shape, sides labelled, find the area | 44 |

Distractor intent per skill (the `near` array): `eqn2` offers the
forgot-to-subtract and forgot-to-divide results; `pythag` offers the sum of
the legs and leg+1; `angle*` offer the other angle and ±10; `expand` offers
b (unmultiplied) and a+b; `simul` offers y and the sum; `indices` offers the
product of exponents; `seqGeo` offers the linear continuation.

## 5. Rendering: two new tokens

The quiz renderer (`quiz.js`) currently knows
`num, balls, op, eq, sep, box, pct, pow, frac`. Two additions:

- **`var`** — `{ t: 'var', v: '3x' }`, rendered as styled (italic) text in
  the question row. `pow` already accepts any value for its base, so `x²` is
  `{ t: 'pow', v: 'x', e: 2 }` with no renderer change.
- **`diag`** — `{ t: 'diag', kind: 'angleLine' | 'angleTri' | 'pythag' |
  'areaComp', ... }` with kind-specific numeric params (the labelled values
  and enough geometry to draw). Rendered as a small inline canvas
  (~180×110 CSS px, drawn at devicePixelRatio) using theme CSS variables for
  stroke and label colours. Labels are numerals and the degree sign only —
  the wordless constraint holds. `maths.js` stays pure: it emits data, and
  only `quiz.js` draws.

The maths lab uses the game's own renderer, so bands 9–11 and their diagrams
appear there with no extra work beyond extending its band loop.

## 6. Adaptive engine changes

Minimal by design:

- `MAX_BAND` becomes a named constant, 8 → 11: `newState` clamp, `pickBand`
  cap, the `_skillIndex` loop, and the band-8 answer floor (`min = -30`)
  becomes "band ≥ 8" (band 9 `eqn2` may not need negatives, but the floor is
  about choice generation, and negatives remain legitimate distractors).
- `expectedMs(band) = 2500 + 900 × band` continues linearly (band 11 ≈
  12.4 s), matching the harder-format intent without a visible timer.
- Acceleration: bands 9–11 use the same damped upward ratio as bands 5–8
  (`ACCEL_UP_OLD_RATIO`) — they hold real ground. Downward acceleration and
  home-band roaming (±2 bands) are unchanged.
- Convergence target stays 80%; the step constants do not change.

## 7. Harder answer format

`choiceCount(difficulty)` currently falls 4 → 3 → 2 at the bottom. It now
also rises at the top: **5 choices at difficulty ≥ 9, 6 at ≥ 11**.
`buildChoices` already prefers the `near` pool and pads with band-scaled
plausible values; it needs to produce up to 6 distinct choices (the `near`
arrays hold 4 entries, so padding covers the rest). The quiz panel's choice
row wraps to accommodate 6 without shrinking tap targets below current size.

## 8. Age row, saves, and placement

- The team editor's age row gains three buttons: **13** → band 9, **14–15**
  → band 10, **16+** → band 11 (continuing age = band + 4, with the last
  button absorbing everything above). `DEFAULT_BAND` stays 3.
- `store.js` repair clamps band to 0–11 instead of 0–8. Existing saves are
  untouched; no migration needed — a band-8 save is still a band-8 save.
- The README band table gains the three rows.

## 9. Pro skin

Keyed off the active team's **home band ≥ 9**, applied as a single class on
`<body>` (e.g. `pro`), implemented as CSS overrides keyed off a single
`body.pro` class (the stylesheet uses no custom properties, so plain
descendant overrides are the variable-free equivalent):

- darker, desaturated palette; the pitch keeps its geometry;
- scoreboard and modals styled like TV match graphics — flat panels, sharper
  corners, no bounce animations;
- celebratory emoji bursts reduced to score flashes.

No layout, markup, or physics changes. A child's team on the same device is
unaffected, per-slot as everything else.

## 10. Form rating and personal bests

- **Rating** = `round(47 + (difficulty − 1) × 52 / 10)`, mapping difficulty
  1 → 47 and 11 → 99, clamped to that range. Shown on the stats card for all
  teams, and in the pro skin's scoreboard area. It is a *display* of the
  engine's difficulty, never an input to it.
- **Personal bests** in `slot.stats`: `bestMs` (fastest correct answer) and
  `bestStreak` (longest correct-answer streak), updated where answers are
  already recorded, repaired like every other stat on read. Shown on the
  stats card.

## 11. Validation

- Generator sweeps per new skill over many seeded draws: answers are whole
  numbers in range, `near` entries are distinct from the answer, diagram
  params are consistent (angles sum to 180, triples satisfy a²+b²=c²,
  L-shape sides consistent with its area).
- Convergence: synthetic learners placed at bands 9–11 settle near 80%
  accuracy, same harness as the existing convergence tests.
- `choiceCount` boundaries: 4 below 9, 5 at 9, 6 at 11; choices distinct and
  ≥ the floor.
- Rating mapping bounds and clamping.
- Mutation-resistance expectation carries over: breaking a constant in the
  new generators must fail the suite.
